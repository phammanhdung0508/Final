KG + GNN Recommendation System

The Recommendation Problem

Users face too many choices. Standard recommendation models can learn behaviour, but they often struggle to explain meaning, intent and why a recommendation fits a specific user.

Too many choices

Streaming platforms, e-commerce and learning systems contain thousands or millions of items.

Sparse history

A user may only rate or interact with a small number of items.

Weak explanation

“Similar users liked this” is useful, but it does not explain semantic fit.

Goal: move from behaviour-only recommendation to semantic, personalized and explainable recommendation.

Why KG alone is not enough

Limitation of Traditional KG

Manual rules

same genre = +1 same theme = +2 same director = +1

But why should these weights be the same for every user?

Mai's real preference weights may differ

Time theme █████████ Humanity ████████ Space ███████ Philosophy ███████ Director ██ Action ▌
A KG stores explicit relationships, but it does not automatically learn which relationship combinations matter most to Mai.

Practical design

Hybrid Recommendation Strategy

A production-style system should not rely on one signal only. Combine explicit KG reasoning with learned GNN prediction.

KG rule score

Shared themes, genres, actors, directors, paths.

GNN score

Learned probability that Mai will like the movie.

Content score

Optional text similarity from descriptions or reviews.

final_score = α × KG_rule_score + β × GNN_prediction_score + γ × content_similarity_score
